About JanSahayak (जनसहायक)

A Senior Capstone AI/ML Research System for Grounded Public Document Intelligence.

Objective & Methodology

JanSahayak addresses information opacity in Indian welfare delivery. Millions of citizens struggle to navigate fragmented portals and 50-page PDF guidelines. JanSahayak indices original government scheme notifications into a hybrid vector store (dense semantic embeddings combined with BM25 lexical keyword matching).

Answers are synthesized using Google Gemini, strictly conditioned on retrieved clauses. If an answer cannot be grounded in official text, the system transparently indicates that the information is not present in its corpus.

Strict Zero-Hallucination Grounding Rule

This is a student research capstone and not an official service of the Government of India or State Governments.
